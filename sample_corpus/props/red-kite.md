# Red Kite

**One line:** Asha's diamond-shaped red paper kite with a long tail of blue ribbons.

## Belongs to / used by
[[asha]] built it from her grandfather's old champion design; [[grandpa-ravi]] keeps the original frame.

## Canon description
Bright red tissue paper on a bamboo cross frame, about one metre tall. A tail of six blue ribbons, each tied with a different knot. A small hand-painted yellow sun sits in the centre.

## Director rules
- The tail always has exactly six ribbons. If one is lost in a story, it is replaced by the end of the episode.
- The kite is never shown torn beyond repair.
