# Grandpa Ravi

**One line:** Asha's grandfather, a retired kite champion who now repairs bicycles and kites in the same small shed.

## Identity locks (never change)
- Tall and thin, white moustache, round steel spectacles on a cord.
- Brown woollen vest over a cream kurta; grey cloth cap.
- Walks with a carved wooden stick he does not really need.

## Personality
Quiet, dry humour, tells stories that start in the middle. He won the valley kite festival seven years in a row and refuses to say how.

## Family and relationships
- Grandfather of [[asha]]; taught her every knot she knows.
- Old rival of the festival judge, a running joke that is never explained.

## Places and props
- Shares [[ashas-workshop]] with Asha; his side holds bicycle parts.
- Keeps the original [[red-kite]] frame he won with, hanging on the wall.

## Director rules
- He never flies a kite on screen any more. He only advises.
