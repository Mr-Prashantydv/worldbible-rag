# Asha

**One line:** 10-year-old kite maker who lives at the foot of [[windy-hill]] with her grandfather [[grandpa-ravi]].

## Identity locks (never change)
- Short curly black hair held back with a yellow headband.
- Orange raincoat with deep pockets full of string, tape and a pencil stub.
- Green rubber boots; she never wears sandals, even in summer.
- Left-handed: she always cuts and ties with her left hand.

## Personality
Patient, stubborn and proud of fixing things herself. She would rather rebuild a broken kite five times than ask for help, and she has to learn, slowly, that asking is not losing.

## Family and relationships
- Lives with [[grandpa-ravi]], who taught her to make kites.
- Best friend: [[bolt]], the neighbour's dog, who follows her everywhere.
- Her parents work in the city and visit on festival days. They are never shown on screen.

## Places and props
- Builds kites in [[ashas-workshop]]; flies them on [[windy-hill]].
- Her best kite is the [[red-kite]].

## Director rules
- Asha never cries in front of other children; she goes to the workshop first.
- Do not give her a phone. The valley story is set before she owns one.
