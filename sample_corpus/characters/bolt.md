# Bolt

**One line:** Scruffy white terrier with one brown ear who belongs to the baker next door but spends every day with [[asha]].

## Identity locks (never change)
- White wiry coat, one brown ear (the left), short stubby tail.
- Wears a blue collar with a round brass tag shaped like a bun.
- Bolt does not talk. He communicates only with barks, head tilts and tail wags.

## Personality
Fast, nosy and easily distracted by birds. Bolt chases every kite string that touches the ground, which has ruined at least three of Asha's launches.

## Family and relationships
- Owner: the baker at the end of the lane (never named).
- Follows [[asha]] and naps under [[grandpa-ravi]]'s chair.

## Director rules
- Bolt is never in danger for comedy. If he runs towards the cliff edge on [[windy-hill]], someone catches him in the same shot.
