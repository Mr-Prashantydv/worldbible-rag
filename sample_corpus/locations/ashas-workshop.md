# Asha's Workshop

**One line:** Small blue-painted wooden shed at the foot of [[windy-hill]], split down the middle between kites and bicycles.

## Canon description
One room with a tin roof and a single round window. Asha's half has a long workbench, bamboo sticks in a bucket and paper sheets pinned to a line. [[grandpa-ravi]]'s half holds two bicycle frames, a tyre pump and a radio that only plays old film songs.

## Notes
- The door sticks; everyone shoulders it open.
- The original [[red-kite]] frame hangs above the window.

## Director rules
- Keep the split visible in wide shots: kites on the left, bicycles on the right.
