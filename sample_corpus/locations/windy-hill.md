# Windy Hill

**One line:** Grassy hill above the valley where everyone flies kites; a single crooked pine tree stands at the top.

## Canon description
A wide green hill with a dirt path winding up its left side. The crooked pine at the summit leans east. A low stone wall marks the cliff edge on the far side, where the valley drops away to the river.

## Fixed geography
- The path always enters frame from the bottom left.
- The pine tree is always at the summit, never halfway down.
- Wind blows from west to east in every scene, so kites always lean right of frame.

## Director rules
- No other buildings on the hill. The workshop is at the bottom, out of shot.
